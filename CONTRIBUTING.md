# Contributing to the community hub

Read the [THD AI contribution guide](https://github.com/THD-AI/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Use Discussions for questions, ideas, introductions, and sharing work. Use issues here for problems with this hub. Course submissions belong in the official submission system. Keep access keys and private information out of public posts.

Historical schedules and screenshots must remain dated. Preserve other people's comments and credit when improving a discussion.
