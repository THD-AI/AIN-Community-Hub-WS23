# THD AI – conversations around the work

![Built with care. Shared with everyone – an informal campus project showcase](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/THD-AI/.github/main/assets/showcase-v1.png)

An independent place for people around THD to share projects, find inspiration, and collaborate in their own time. Personal work, university experiments, and useful things belong alongside each other. AI-assisted or made without AI, quality, care, and honest credit matter.

| Start here | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Project showroom](https://thd-ai.github.io/) | Explore the work and the people behind it. |
| [Introductions](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/1) | Say what interests you and what you want to make. |
| [Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/categories/ideas) | Find collaborators, even before you have a finished project. |
| [Show and tell](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) | Share a result, a work in progress, or something you learned. |
| [Q&A](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/categories/q-a) | Ask for help and help someone else. |
| [Share a project in the showroom](https://github.com/THD-AI/thd-ai.github.io/issues/new?template=project-submission.yml) | Submit a reviewed listing; the repository can stay in your account. |

Members can create and lead public or private repositories. Public contributions, discussions, and showroom submissions do not require membership. See [how we work](https://github.com/THD-AI/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
